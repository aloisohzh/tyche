Controls

Operator settings. Choose how orders are approved, arm or release the kill-switch, and see where every figure comes from. Visitors see this page read-only - only the operator can change anything, and everyone can refresh.

Operator console

This desk is shared read-only. Unlock with your operator passphrase to change the trading mode or the kill-switch. The passphrase is held in memory only and never stored.

Trading mode -

Kill-switch

Stops new buys right away; sells that reduce risk still go through. Every change is recorded in the journal and reaches the cloud desk.

Refresh

Fresh data loads when you open the desk or press Refresh. Nothing polls in the background - figures stay put until you ask for new ones.

1-5 views · R refresh · T theme · Esc close

Telegram alerts

The cloud desk pushes risk alerts and digests to Telegram (@tyche_sentinel_bot) - no PC needed. Order approval is not sent through Telegram; you approve at this desk, per order or with a pre-approved envelope.

Controls

Operator settings in one place. The approval envelope, the strategy switches, the scheduled checks, Scout and the kill-switch are grouped below and stay locked for visitors - unlock with the operator passphrase to change them. Data sources and known gaps are listed underneath for everyone.

Trading mode -

Strategies in play

Turn a playbook off and the desk stops opening new positions in it; exits, stops and targets keep running, so nothing gets stranded. Scout only proposes for what is left on.

Scheduled daily checks

The desk runs itself in the cloud - pre-market, a midday watch and the close - even when this PC is off. Here is the cadence that owns today, what it last did, and the switch for it.

Run Scout

Starts the researcher on its own, right now, over the playbooks that are live. It reads sources and writes trade ideas for you to review - it cannot size, approve or submit an order, and nothing it says reaches the broker without going through the audit and approval path.

Kill-switch

Stops new buys right away; sells that reduce risk still go through. Every change is recorded in the journal and reaches the cloud desk.

  • Blocksnew buy orders everywhere - desk, executor and cloud runs
  • Still passessells that reduce existing risk
  • Recorded inthe journal plus the shared halt flag the cloud desk reads
Read-only for visitors

The approval envelope, the strategy switches, the daily checks, Scout and the kill-switch cannot be changed while locked. Unlock above to make changes; viewing always stays open.

Desk information

Where every figure comes from, and the gaps we would rather state than hide.

Refresh

Fresh data loads when you open the desk or press Refresh in the top-right corner. Nothing polls in the background - figures stay put until you ask for new ones.

1-5 views · R refresh · T theme · Esc close

Telegram alerts

The cloud desk pushes risk alerts and digests to Telegram (@tyche_sentinel_bot) - no PC needed. Order approval is not sent through Telegram; you approve at this desk, per order or with a pre-approved envelope.

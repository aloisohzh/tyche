Controls & administration

Operator-only settings, grouped away from the read-only desk. Trading mode (manual per-order confirmation, auto with your last approved bounds, or a fresh pre-approved envelope), the kill-switch, refresh behavior, the outbound Telegram sentinel, and the honest data-source / divergence record.

Trading mode -

Kill-switch

Blocks new buys immediately; de-risking sells still pass. Every transition is journaled and auditable.

Refresh

The desk loads fresh data on open and whenever you refresh. There is no background polling - figures stay put until you ask for new ones.

Refresh now
Re-reads journal state + Forward automation. Also in the top bar, or press R.

Telegram sentinel · outbound only

Push alerts from the cloud desk. Order approval is not routed through Telegram.

Outbound: working. Schedule runs push risk alerts + digests via the Bot API (@tyche_sentinel_bot) - no PC required, the cloud desk sends them.
Inbound approval: skipped by operator decision (2026-11-19). The desk PC is not always on, so a local approval listener cannot be relied on. Orders are approved at the desk only: per-order confirmation, or a bounded pre-approved envelope for unattended runs.

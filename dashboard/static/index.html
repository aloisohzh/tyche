My desk at a glance

What my practice account is worth, whether I am up or down, and how much risk I am taking. Every number comes from the broker or the desk's own record book.

How to read this page
  • What my account is worth: all my cash plus all my holdings at today's prices. The line under it shows how far I am from the $100,000 I started with.
  • How am I doing: Total is my overall result. Open is on things I still hold, so it can still change. Banked is from trades I have finished. Click Banked or Win rate to see those trades.
  • Account value over time: a dot for every time the account was checked. The desk checks a few times a day, so the line moves in steps.
  • Is the market friendly? Four quick tests. More ticks means a safer time to buy. The emergency stop stops all buying when it is on.
  • What I own comes straight from the broker. Weight is how big a slice each holding is. No slice may be bigger than 40%.
  • How fresh is this? The top bar says when the numbers were published. A red bar at the top means they are older than the last trading day.

Trade ideas and results

Before the desk can trade, each idea must answer five questions. Click any row to see the answers, the stop and target, and the proof behind it. If something is missing, it says so.

Complete Partial Missing
How to read this page
  • Trade ideas: every trade the desk thought about, newest first. Status says what happened: Filled means it was done. Approved means it passed the checks but was not sent. Blocked means a rule stopped it. Not sent means a later order took its place.
  • Checks: before a trade can happen the desk must answer five questions. Why this trade? What proof is there? Is the market friendly? Which stock or fund? How much should it make? The number shows how many have answers. Click a row to see them.
  • Max loss: the most this trade should lose if it goes wrong. It is a limit, not a guess at what will happen.
  • Is the plan working? This asks whether the trades make money on average. We need 30 finished trades before this means anything. Right now there are 3, so a 100% win rate is luck, not skill.
  • Finished trades: each trade from buy to sell, with what it cost and what it made. These are the trades behind Banked and Win rate on the Overview.
  • How complete is the paperwork? For each trade: what was written down, what the desk worked out, and what is still missing.

Safety and orders

The emergency stop, the rules the desk must obey, every order it sent, and every idea the rules blocked. Sending an order is not the same as it being done; only the broker can confirm that.

How to read this page
  • Emergency stop: Off means trading is allowed. On means no more buying; only selling to reduce risk is allowed. The list under it is every time it was switched.
  • How close to the limits: each bar is one rule and the small mark is the limit. Keep holdings under 95% of the account, keep cash above 5%, and no single holding above 40%.
  • Drawdown ladder: how far the account has dropped from its best value. At 4% down I get a warning. At 6% down the desk stops buying by itself. The daily stop is a 3% loss in one day.
  • The rules: every rule the desk must obey, checked against the latest numbers. OK means fine. Not measurable means the desk does not have the data to check it right now.
  • Orders: every order the desk asked for. Filled means the broker did it. Submitted means it was sent and we are waiting. Expired means an old order from another day that was never filled.
  • Trades the rules blocked: ideas that broke a rule before reaching the broker. This list is good news. It shows the rules working.

When the desk runs

The desk runs by itself in the cloud, three times every trading day, even when my computer is off. It never buys anything new without my yes on Telegram, unless I set limits for the day.

Connecting…
How to read this page
  • The desk runs three times every trading day. At 21:05 Singapore time it makes a plan. At 01:05 it checks every holding against its stop and target. At 04:05 it trades. Between runs nobody is watching the market.
  • Market calendar: the parts of the US trading day and what the desk may do in each. The days ahead show holidays, half days and big news events that can move prices.
  • The timer card: the alarm clock that wakes the desk. There is one card. It rings three times every weekday, and the panel shows each ring in New York and Singapore time.
  • Every run: a log of each time the desk woke up and what it cost in cloud credits. Test runs and read-only checks are hidden behind a button.
  • The team: the coordinator makes the plan and talks to me on Telegram. The researcher finds ideas. The auditor checks every plan. The executor is the only one allowed to place orders, and only after approval.

Controls

The switches: trading mode, which strategies are on, the timer, Scout and the emergency stop. Anyone can look; only the owner with the password can press the buttons.

How to read this page
  • Trading mode: Ask me means every new trade waits for my yes on Telegram. Within my bounds means the desk may trade inside limits I set for one day without asking.
  • Strategies in play: a strategy that is Off starts no new trades. Selling to protect existing holdings keeps working.
  • Scheduled daily checks: the timer card that owns today, when it runs next and what the last runs did. Run Scout starts a research-only run that cannot place orders.
  • Emergency stop: stops all new buying right away, everywhere. Selling to reduce risk still works.

About the numbers

Where every number comes from, and what is not finished yet.

Refresh and shortcuts

Refresh asks for the newest published numbers. It cannot make new ones: the numbers come from the desk's private journal, and a publish script copies a safe version of them to this site. Until that script runs on its own after every desk run, a person has to run it. The top bar shows when the numbers were last published.

Telegram alerts

The cloud desk pushes risk alerts and digests to Telegram (@tyche_sentinel_bot) - no PC needed. In Ask me mode the desk also sends each entry proposal here during the fire and waits for your /approve N; in Within my bounds it trades inside the bounds you armed at this desk without asking.
